# AI Sales Agent Showcase Talking Points

Use these lines during demo recording/presentation.

## Opening (20-30s)

1. This is a visible AI-first sales workspace, not hidden automation.
2. The flow is deterministic for demos, auditable for trust, and modular for production extension.
3. Each AI action writes timeline evidence and can be inspected in logs.

## AI Copilot

1. Copilot supports intent-driven actions: follow-ups, draft reply, and at-risk deal insights.
2. Output is structured and validated, with fallback safety when model outputs fail.
3. Actions can run in safe mode and produce audit events.

## Lead Intelligence + Triage

1. Triage turns raw lead text into actionable intelligence: intent, urgency, complexity, quantity, confidence, score.
2. Intelligence is persistent and separate from one-time execution output.
3. Next-best-action is explainable and traceable.

## Reply Studio

1. Reply drafts are context-aware (lead/deal/quote) and tone-controlled.
2. Human-in-the-loop is built in: edit, regenerate, approve-and-send.
3. Every send path is auditable.

## Quote Copilot

1. Quote recommendation suggests products/variants and detects missing data.
2. Quote Copilot summarizes accepted lines and adds upsell/cross-sell guidance.
3. Quote intelligence includes margin safety and discount guidance.

## Pipeline + Flow + Automation

1. Pipeline Insights prioritizes risk and urgency with one-click actions.
2. Agent Flow provides step-level status, blockers, evidence, and next move.
3. Automation Runs show execution history, failure metadata, and rerun guardrails.

## KPI Close (30-45s)

1. AI Impact KPIs make results measurable: time saved, suggestions accepted, risk alerts resolved.
2. The same KPI contract is visible in AI tab and Dashboard for leadership visibility.
3. Baseline screenshots and deterministic setup ensure repeatable portfolio demos.

## Optional Freelancer Pitch Ending

1. This demo shows both product UX and production-minded reliability.
2. I can adapt this architecture to your CRM stack, workflows, and approval model.
3. Delivery includes deterministic demo mode, QA checklist, and measurable AI impact instrumentation.
